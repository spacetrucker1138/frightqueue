Steam Sale & Fest Planner for Horror Devs

Every upcoming Steam seasonal sale, themed fest and Next Fest Valve has announced, with the dates you need to hit so the 30-day cooldowns don't lock you out. Horror-relevant events are marked 🎃.

The 30-day rules in plain English

  • Release cooldown: no discounts for 30 days after release (Early Access and full release both count). The only exception is an optional launch discount (7 to 14 days, max 40%), which must be set up before release.
  • Discount cooldown: a discount can't start within 30 days of the end of your previous discount. This covers Daily/Midweek/Weekend/Weeklong deals, Steam Fests, publisher sales and custom discounts.
  • Seasonal sales are the exception: the Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter sales are exempt from the discount cooldown, so you can run a discount just before or after one and still join. They are not exempt from the release or price-increase cooldowns.
  • Price-increase cooldown: no discounts for 30 days after raising your base price in any currency.
  • Fests don't require a discount: themed fests prioritise discounted games, but free, Coming Soon and demo games can take part, and you can be featured at full price.
  • Discounts run 1 to 14 days at 10% to 95% off. A 20%+ discount can trigger wishlist emails.

Source: Steamworks: Discounting.
